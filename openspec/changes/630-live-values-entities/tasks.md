## Workflow follow-up

- The finisher writes two changesets, one for each row of "Package Impact" with a bump: `@thrashplay/wishem-core` minor and `@thrashplay/wishem-construct-manager` minor.
- The final reviewer does each step of "The test guide" of design.md, runs `render.mjs --check`, and runs the type check of the dependents of `core` (`...^@thrashplay/wishem-core`, after a build).
- Before the archive, read the main specs of the MODIFIED requirements on `main` again (`wishem/core/store` "An input adds an entity", `wishem/workbench/construct-manager` "The construct manager sends each change of the product model" and "A step back keeps the time state and the authored changes"), and copy each scenario that a different change added.
- After the merge, create the one break-test issue of the change: the source of `core` and `construct-manager` (library packages), and the tests that the change added or changed, and create the issues of "Follow-up" of design.md.

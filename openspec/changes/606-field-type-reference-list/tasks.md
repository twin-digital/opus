## Workflow follow-up

- The final reviewer does each step of "The test guide" of design.md on the branch, runs `node nodejs/wishem/docs/architecture/render.mjs --check`, and runs the type check of `...^@thrashplay/wishem-core` after a build.
- The finisher writes the seven changesets of design.md, "Package Impact", adds a comment on #557 for its `isEqual` item, creates the follow-up issues of design.md, "Follow-up", and archives the change with the sync of its specs. Before the archive, read the main specs of `wishem/core/store` and `wishem/workbench/product-panel` on `main` again, because #607 can change them.
- After the merge, the lead agent creates the break-test issue for `core` and `renderer-react`, which are library packages.

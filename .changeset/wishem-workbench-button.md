Give the `Button` component the variants `maker`, `neutral`, `quiet`, and `danger`, a loading state, and each native `button` prop. The label is `children`, `onClick` replaces `onPress`, `loading` replaces `busy`, and `neutral` replaces the variant `plain`. A disabled button has the native `disabled` attribute, and the prop `focusableWhenDisabled` keeps the focus with `aria-disabled`. `accessibleName` stays.

# Brand identity

> Planning seed, not a specification. It collects what the blueprint knows about this topic, as the start of its brainstorm. The folder name is the expected name of the OpenSpec change. See `../../README.md` for the status labels.

## Goal

This topic is the parts of the Wishem brand that are not decided. It is not on the roadmap.

## What is agreed

[`brand.md`](../../brand.md) holds the brand basics:

- the colors, with one color for the designer and one for the agent
- the type
- the tone of the agent, and the words it uses
- the asides from Wishem, and where they are permitted
- the story of the name

## Open questions

1. **What is the logo mark?** The options from the brand basics are a spark, a lantern, and a small clay figure.
2. **What is the illustration style?**
3. **How does the agent's activity move on the screen?** The brand gives the agent the color Wisp. The motion is not designed. Also for: [Tools](../add-designer-tools/README.md).

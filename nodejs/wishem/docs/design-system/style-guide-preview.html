Recordings and reruns

Wishem ran your level 40 times overnight. Two runs got stuck behind the gate in room 3, and the replays are ready. Every run can be stepped, recorded, replayed and rewound, so the same starting state and the same inputs always give the same result.

When you miss a jump

When you miss a jump, say so. The spirit runs your exact inputs again with a little more height, and reports what it changed, what it tested, and what it is not sure about.
